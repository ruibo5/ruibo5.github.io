I am Ruibo Duan, currently a Ph.D. student at the Department of Computer Science and Engineering (CSE), Seoul National University (SNU). I received my master's degree in Electrical Engineering from Korea University. Before that, I obtained my bachelor's degree in Computer Science from Yonsei University.

My research interests lie at the intersection of large language models (LLMs), evolutionary computation, and combinatorial optimization. My research aims to develop automated optimization frameworks that integrate language-model-based reasoning, adaptive search, and algorithm generation for solving complex optimization problems. I study and develop methods for 1) characterizing the structure of complex optimization landscapes, 2) synthesizing adaptive algorithmic strategies through iterative feedback, and 3) enhancing the robustness and generalization of optimization systems under challenging and heterogeneous problem distributions.

Feel free to drop me an email if you are interested in my research or would like to explore potential collaborations :)
